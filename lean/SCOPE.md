# Formal scope and written deductions

The formal results concern the exact fixed-template uniform polynomial families and polynomial targets defined in the sources. For the classification and lifting results the coefficient field has positive prime characteristic; field, template, support, degree, onset and rank conditions are visible in the full returned types. An axiom report does not discharge a theorem's hypotheses. Inspect the definitions as well as the endpoint type.

- **Refutation profiles:** `LinearFinal.linear_classification` supplies the established NS and ordinary-PC linear-bound route for actual uniform templates. `PaperAudit` exposes the degree-zero, arbitrary admissible period, T2 extended-order, T3 residue, T4 peak, common-type/comparison and concrete witness forms used in the paper. Universe size is the parameter `n`.
- **Invariant targets:** `UniformConsequence.invariant_target_endpoint` concerns the actual bounded-degree invariant polynomial target over a fixed template. `target_transfer` and the scope adapters retain the explicit strict prime-power range, rank onset, degree-zero cases, floors and additive NS derivation convention. `uniform_inclusion` and `uniform_derivation_comparison` concern actual derivations and preserve the stated degree convention.
- **Indexed targets:** `UniformConsequence.Indexed.indexed_target_endpoint` concerns bounded-support indexed polynomial targets over fixed index data. The transfer budget is `2*k*d+s+t`, not a claimed sharp maximum bound. The positive-degree onset is `max(nI, 4*k*d+2*(s+t)+1)`; degree zero has its separate onset. `orbit_endpoint` concerns one fixed global orbit and a representative within it. It does not permit arbitrary switching between unrelated orbits as rank varies.
- **Fresh variable:** `UniformConsequence.Fresh.tpcDegree_y` and `tnsDegree_y` give the exact ordinary-PC `max(1,d_PC)` and NS `d_NS+1` identities over arbitrary fields/axiom sets, including degree zero and infinity. The preserved `bhat` fresh-target witness has finite hard values and T4 behaviour in its stated positive-characteristic scope. It is not a claim of a satisfiable hard axiom-system witness.
- **Comparison:** `PC <= NS <= F(PC)` uses a monotone degree function independent of universe size, with the stated parameters. It gives agreement of types and the specified common peaks. It does not assert equality of NS and PC degrees in general.
- **Four types:** T1 is bounded within its eventual scope; T2 gives eventual linear hardness, with finite-value qualifications where a finite degree is required; T3 separates eventual easy residue classes from complementary hard classes; T4 supplies finite linear peaks and finite liminf. T4 is not universal hardness at every sufficiently large size. General linear lower bounds do not themselves establish a universal linear upper bound.
- **Period:** the general bound is `Q <= p*R`; equality is possible. Keep the strict prime-power convention in transfer arguments distinct from this non-strict period bound.
- **Undecidability:** the exact NS/PC union, pair and disjoint-set endpoints use the encoded first-order syntax, effective translations and promise hypotheses printed by `UAudit`. The no-finite-model promise is part of the published LICS 2008 baseline. The current controlled-join reductions refine that formulation with explicit pair separation; formal execution does not decide exhaustive historical priority.

The following remain written mathematics or correspondence/presentation judgments: the injection `n+1` upper certificate and asymptotic optimality deduction; degree-to-size presentations and size corollaries beyond their explicitly formalised ingredients; all exposition, worked traces, teaching exercises, and the claim that a displayed manuscript sentence matches an endpoint. Related Lean declarations do not automatically certify those deductions.
