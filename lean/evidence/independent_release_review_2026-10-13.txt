Corrections and limitations of Claude's audit
============================================
1. AI disclosure: the paper already explicitly credits Astra and Claude for sustained proof search, formalisation, verification support and exposition, and records human direction and responsibility. Claude's summary reduces this to exposition and thereby overstates the deficiency. Different paper/book disclosure lengths are not themselves an inconsistency.
2. Harman: tensor/natural-operation compatibility already appears in the earlier paper; see A above. This correction affects attribution, not the validity of the independent construction.
3. Period: audit report line 572 says 'period < pR'. The general bound is Q <= pR, with equality possible when R is a prime power. The actual manuscript already has the correct inequality; do not introduce the audit's mistake into it.
4. TSV: its Boolean-completeness row assigns paper pp. 15–16, but the relevant paper text is on pp. 10–11. The mathematical correspondence is unaffected, but the location should be corrected.
5. Package inventory: the paper ZIP has 24 audit entries, rather than the audit's reported 26. The underlying public-packet hygiene concern is valid.
6. Commands: some Lean commands already appear on the site. The missing item is complete pinned reproducibility, not total absence of commands.
7. Browser JSON: the recorded deep-link anchor top is -2337 and does not establish correct positioning below the header. Reduced-motion and scroll-restoration checks are not recorded. There are no contrast, zoom or screen-reader results. A 27-character selection with one panel is not by itself proof that selection opened no panel. Static identity/link validation is separate from sampled interactive browser coverage. Retain these distinctions in any readiness claim.
8. The alleged three conflicting textbook routes are purpose-specific reading orders; no logical contradiction was established. Hardest-step pedagogy remains a real concern.
9. No mathematical blocker found is an evidence-based audit conclusion, not a guarantee of absolute correctness or universal novelty. Neither returned clean builds nor formalisation labels certify all presentation and derived-size claims.
