# Archive and licence metadata

This source packet is included in version 1.0.0 of the paper and supporting-material archive, DOI https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23965838. The author approved CC BY 4.0 on 5 November 2026; see the root LICENSE. Upstream Lean, Mathlib and dependency licences remain unchanged. Their sources/binaries are not bundled here.

Public execution evidence consists of explicitly labelled derivatives with historical machine roots replaced by declared placeholders. Original/public hashes and exact provenance are recorded for each copy. Types, axiom reports, exit statuses and proof content are preserved. The unsanitised original records remain unchanged in the existing project archives and are not bundled in the public packet. Newly authored instructions and manifests use relative archive paths. Proof sources and pinned configuration remain byte-identical to their originals.
