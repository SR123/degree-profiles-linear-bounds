# Execution evidence and its provenance

Execution files under `evidence/` are public derivatives of preserved records. Historical machine roots are replaced by declared placeholders; no theorem type, axiom report, exit-status line or other proof content is altered. Some files require no substitution, but all execution files are labelled public evidence copies. The unchanged unsanitised originals remain at the archive-relative project locations recorded in `provenance/source_origins.json` and are not bundled here. `provenance/evidence_redactions.json` records each original/public SHA256, mapping and replacement count; `SHA256SUMS.txt` covers the public bytes. These records are not newly run commands. Exact verbatim claims concern proof sources, pinned configuration and the explicitly identified independent correction review below.

The [independent release review of 13 October](evidence/independent_release_review_2026-10-13.txt) is included verbatim, with original/public hash equality and archive provenance. It corrects the producer report's `period < pR` typo to `Q <= pR`, its overstated AI-disclosure deficiency, its attribution of natural-operation compatibility to the new construction, and its claims about browser/accessibility evidence. It also preserves the exact formal/written and replay-scope distinctions. This review inspected returned evidence and sources; it performed no new Lean execution or kernel replay. These corrections are part of the public interpretation of the producer evidence.
