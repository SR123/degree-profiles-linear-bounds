MAIN CHANGES
1. The size threshold is N(d), matching the paper; the unrelated quadratic constant A and indexed algebraic quantities are retained. A_d(h) is explicitly a residue set, not a threshold. The two different uses of N (onset N(d), universe size N=n+q) are explained.
2. New front-matter reading route links difficult questions to the appropriate chapters, including bounded-degree spaces, transport of derivations, the linear improvement, target survival, and verification scope.
3. Chapter 18 now begins with the hardness spectrum for all uniform requests covered by the theorem, without a Boolean-encoding restriction. The conventions at d=0, prime-power periods, eventual thresholds, and NS/PC comparison agree with the paper.
4. The four topological shapes are proved: empty (T1), full (T2), nonempty proper clopen (T3), and closed but not open (T4). A residue-tree explanation supplies the needed compactness argument.
5. The abstract example h(n)=2^v_2(n) shows how every positive finite size can have finite degree while the limiting hard spectrum is {0}. It is explicitly a toy profile, not a claimed realisation by a polynomial family. The logarithmic profile 1+v_2(n) has the same spectrum but demonstrates why topology alone does not prove linear growth. The linear onset and period estimates are essential.
6. The spectrum is attributed to the residue-class structure already implicit in the 2009 report, Section 4, pages 18–19. No new four-way classification or full Euler-model converse is claimed.
7. Section 20.7 explains the relationship to representation stability, exactness and reflection, rook operators, designs, and p-adic topology. Analogies are distinguished from imported theorems.
8. All 28 bibliography entries were checked for identity, publication/version metadata and their stated role, using primary publisher, author, institutional, or repository records where available. The separate compression companion remains explicitly unpublished. The PC-foundation publisher text was not accessible in this review; its bibliographic record was corroborated from the authors' research literature. No new claim depends on inaccessible details of that paper.
